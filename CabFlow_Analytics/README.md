# CabFlow Analytics (July 2024)

A Power BI project analyzing 103,024 cab ride bookings to understand booking performance,
vehicle-type economics, revenue, cancellations, and service ratings.

## Dashboard pages

1. **Overall** — total bookings, total booking value, booking status breakdown, ride volume trend
2. **Vehicle Type** — booking value, success value, and distance by vehicle category
3. **Revenue** — revenue by payment method, top 5 customers, daily distance trend
4. **Cancellation** — cancellation rate and reason breakdown (customer-initiated vs. driver-initiated)
5. **Ratings** — driver and customer ratings by vehicle type

## Quick summary

- 103,024 bookings, ₹35M total booking value, 62.09% success rate
- 28.08% overall cancellation rate — driver-side issues (cancellations + "Driver Not Found") outweigh customer cancellations
- Auto rides average 10 km vs. ~25 km for every other vehicle type — a distinct short-trip segment
- Cash and UPI dominate payments over cards
- Ratings are consistently ~4.0 across all vehicle types, both driver and customer side

See `CabFlow_Analytics_Case_Study.md` for the full breakdown and methodology.
